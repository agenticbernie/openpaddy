# OpenPaddy PoC — 3-Minute Demo Video Script (UTC Startup 2027)

**Target Audience:** Startup competition judges, rice export enterprise executives, agricultural cooperative leaders.  
**Core Hypothesis:** Export enterprises will adopt a compliance-data-driven orchestration platform to reduce contract breaches and post-harvest losses, even when field data is manually entered.

---

## 0:00 – 0:35 | 1. PAIN (The Mekong Delta Bottleneck)
- **Visual:** Slide or split-screen showing Mekong Delta rice fields + messy Zalo/Excel screenshots.
- **Narration:**
  "Vietnam's Mekong Delta exports millions of tons of rice each year, yet export enterprises lose up to 15% of contract value to side-selling, missed AWD drainage schedules, and unverified sustainability claims. Today, cooperatives track compliance in Zalo chats and paper notebooks—leaving exporters blind until harvest day."

## 0:35 – 1:15 | 2. SOLUTION (OpenPaddy Trust-First Orchestration)
- **Visual:** Live screen recording of OpenPaddy `/login` → `/contracts`.
- **Narration:**
  "Meet OpenPaddy: a trust-first orchestration platform built for real-world field conditions. An export enterprise creates a digital contract in 30 seconds with clear compliance rules—like 2 AWD drains and 1 straw management check-in—and the cooperative signs with a single tap."
- **Action:** Show `Tạo hợp đồng` modal, add/view rules, and show `Đã ký` badge.

## 1:15 – 2:00 | 3. VALIDATION (3-Step Field Check-in & Transparent Geo-Trust)
- **Visual:** Mobile viewport of `/check-in`.
- **Narration:**
  "In the field, cooperative officers don't need satellites or AI to build trust. In 3 thumb-friendly steps, they select the parcel, snap a photo, and OpenPaddy validates GPS within 100 meters of the parcel centroid—giving instant, plain Vietnamese feedback: '✓ Vị trí khớp (±15m)'. Even offline, check-ins queue safely."
- **Action:** Select parcel, upload photo preview, show green geo-check badge, submit, and show checkmark confirmation.

## 2:00 – 2:40 | 4. HARVEST ORCHESTRATION & GREEN DOSSIER PDF
- **Visual:** `/dashboard` with MapLibre polygons, status cards, and PDF download.
- **Narration:**
  "Back at HQ, the exporter sees every parcel color-coded in real time—red for missing compliance, amber for ripening, green for harvest-ready—with rule-based harvest date estimates they can adjust. With one click on 'Tạo dossier', OpenPaddy generates a client-side Green Dossier PDF with compliance records, a map snapshot, and a 24-hour presigned traceability QR link."
- **Action:** Click `Tạo dossier`, show downloaded PDF and presigned URL.

## 2:40 – 3:00 | 5. VISION & ASK
- **Visual:** Dashboard "Satellite & AI Verification — Coming Soon" card + UTC Startup 2027 closing slide.
- **Narration:**
  "Once manual compliance workflows are proven and trusted, Sentinel-2 satellite and AI verification plug directly into this same pipeline. We're raising seed support and piloting with 2 Mekong Delta export enterprises for the 2027 season. Thank you!"
